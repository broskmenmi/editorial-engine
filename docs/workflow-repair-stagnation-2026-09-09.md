# Editorial workflow repair — stagnation guardrails

Authorized by the owner on 2026-09-09 after STRANGE GAIT produced a 31-run canonical ADD drought while the global Discovery Pool continued to grow rapidly.

This file records the process diagnosis only. It is not a musical verdict and does not change any playlist ledger.

## Diagnosis

The workflow had developed two reinforcing biases:

1. Scout increasingly treated strong track-specific prose plus an already-defensible neighbour placement as a practical prerequisite for resolver admission. That filtered out too much material before evaluation.
2. Evaluator/Auditor repeatedly treated missing direct transition/audio evidence as if UNKNOWN meant FAIL, creating an epistemic veto even when the best available evidence could support a provisional editorial placement.

The observed symptom was not merely a low ADD count. It was a funnel problem: large inspected scopes collapsing to very few leads, many exact interesting tracks retained outside the canonical playlist, and recurring PARK decisions with the same missing-handoff rationale.

## Repair principles

- Scout should find credible contenders, not prove their final placement.
- A lead needs a plausible belonging hypothesis and at least one lawful placement hypothesis, but does not need direct evidence that the handoff works before resolution.
- Direct track-specific rhythmic prose is valuable but not mandatory for lead admission when reliable contextual evidence is sufficient to justify serious evaluation.
- UNKNOWN is not negative evidence. Missing audio, phrase, perceived-pulse or mix evidence must remain UNKNOWN and may coexist with a provisional ADD when the best available evidence supports a deliberate placement at transition, local-run and long-form scale.
- REVISIT is for decision-critical uncertainty, not for every missing measurement.
- The resolver may still select at most three exact candidates. Scout should therefore optimize those slots for canonical potential and journey usefulness, not conspicuous novelty.
- No ADD quota is introduced. Stagnation metrics are diagnostic guardrails only.
- Every RUN ANALYSIS must expose inspection-to-lead conversion, lead-to-resolution conversion, candidate-to-ADD conversion, consecutive completed runs since the last ADD, and retained exact discoveries since the last ADD when available.
- Sustained low conversion plus repeated identical blocker language must trigger workflow-health scrutiny and, when the Auditor identifies an actionable process defect, REPAIR before another ordinary EXPLORE.

The binding rules live in AGENTS.md, the Agent Skills and each target automation.md. This document preserves the reason for the repair so future edits do not silently reintroduce the same bias.
