# DISCOVERY POOL — Utility Contract

`playlists/discovery-pool/` is a global Editorial Engine utility playlist, not a canonical volume.

## Purpose

Retain exact Spotify tracks that an editorial discovery run judged genuinely worth keeping, regardless of whether they became a canonical ADD, a REVISIT/PARKED candidate, or a placement-specific REJECT that remained musically interesting.

## Rules

- No doctrine or volume constitution applies to musical belonging here.
- No sequencing, transition, chapter, journey, BPM-flow, peak, wave, role-diversity, artist-diversity, or long-form tests apply.
- Row order in `ledger.md` is deterministic append history only. It has no musical meaning.
- Spotify-side sorting is a presentation choice and must not be treated as canonical order.
- Exact Spotify identity is required before publication.
- Release watches, research leads, unresolved identities, duplicates, and resolver failures do not enter the Spotify ledger.
- A track may coexist here and in one or more canonical playlists.
- Canonical rejection does not automatically remove a retained track from this pool.
- Listener-supplied tracks do not enter merely because they appear in a canonical playlist; they require an independent discovery-retention decision or explicit user instruction.
- Remove only for wrong/duplicate identity, explicit user instruction, or another objective data-integrity correction.

## Lifecycle

This utility has no dedicated recurring editorial task, no journey map, no sequencing workflow, and no listener-feedback repair chain. It is populated as a side effect of audited discovery runs across all current and future canonical volumes.
