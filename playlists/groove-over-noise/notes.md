# GROOVE OVER NOISE — Editorial Notes

## Operating rules

- The workflow exists to make discovery relaxing.
- No listening homework or required comparisons.
- BPM guides placement but does not prove listening quality.
- Volunteered feedback overrides editorial theory.
- A complaint opens a collaborative clarification; it does not authorize a playlist change.
- Repeated skipping, stress, dislike, relief, and abruptness are strong evidence but still require clarification unless removal is explicit.
- Any repair affecting more than the complained-about track requires named scope and explicit approval.
- The first track defines the playlist's identity, not merely its starting tempo.
- Preserve one dominant summit and the final descent.
- Outward discovery does not require a pre-existing defect; admission still requires a distinct function and an auditable non-frozen placement.
- `MANUAL ACTION` is technical only.

## Authoritative feedback rule

Read `feedback-protocol.md` before acting on listener feedback.

While a discussion is `AWAITING CLARIFICATION`:

- freeze the affected region;
- keep that region's order and membership unchanged;
- ask at most three short questions answerable from memory;
- do not reject, restore, move, replace, remove, or add neighbouring tracks there;
- continue autonomous discovery elsewhere.
