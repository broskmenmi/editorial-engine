# GROOVE OVER NOISE — Felt Journey Map Specification

Repository-root `FELT-VISUALIZATION-DOCTRINE.md` is the default visualization contract and supersedes the older story-curve-first presentation.

## Purpose

Make the long-form listening experience legible as **movement through pressure, groove, release and attention**, while keeping the existing wave/chapter interpretation available as a secondary structure lens.

## Default view

Use a **felt direction ribbon**, not a scalar energy curve.

Show:

- exact canonical order across elapsed time;
- evidence-bound direction phrases;
- transition vectors;
- selected-track context with two tracks before and two after where available;
- protected, accepted, provisional and frozen state;
- switchable Feeling, Groove, Structure and Tempo views.

Unknown felt dimensions remain unknown.

## Structure lens

Arrival, waves, crests, summit, decompression and dissolution remain valid editorial structure vocabulary. They are useful for explanation, but they are no longer the default visual axis and must never masquerade as measured energy.

## Tempo

BPM and duration remain a separate measured metadata layer.

## Compact map

`playlists/groove-over-noise/journey-map.svg` is a compact direction ribbon by default:

- elapsed time is horizontal;
- direction language is qualitative and provenance-bound;
- wave/chapter state is secondary;
- BPM is separate;
- protected/frozen regions remain visible;
- no vertical coordinate represents energy.

## Visual identity

- black and charcoal base;
- graphite texture;
- steel-grey typography;
- restrained deep blue for protected elements;
- amber only for unresolved discussion;
- architectural, bodily, precise and spacious;
- no neon cyberpunk;
- no generic analytics-dashboard styling.

## Evidence boundary

Felt and groove language follows `FELT-VISUALIZATION-DOCTRINE.md`. Editorial wave language is clearly editorial. BPM/duration are metadata. Unknown audio qualities remain unknown.
