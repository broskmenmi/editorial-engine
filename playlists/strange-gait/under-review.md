## Resolved reviews

### 2026-09-06 — Opening vibe / energy continuity

**Affected region before repair:**

`Marauder → Some Letters → Alive → Drift C`

**Listener report:**

> “I'm having a hard time with the energy shift in each track. On paper, it sounded fun, but the weird shift makes it look like a beginner dj has created a Playlist of his favorite tracks but has no taste in creating a vibe.”

**Original editorial model:**

The opening assigned successive tracks distinct conceptual functions — Ground extension, Tilt and Lock — and treated close BPM plus different rhythmic mechanisms as evidence that the story was progressing.

**Shared diagnosis proposed:**

The sequence optimized for role diversity and rhythmic novelty before protecting one coherent vibe/energy envelope. The chapter taxonomy looked intentional on paper, but the listener experienced the result as track-by-track energy resets and a loss of taste/cohesion.

**Named repair scope proposed to the user:**

- keep Inigo Kennedy — Marauder as the only provisional opening anchor;
- remove Aaron Schwarz — Some Letters;
- remove IGLO — Alive;
- remove Stanislav Tolkachev — Drift C;
- rewrite STRANGE GAIT so vibe/energy continuity is the first admission gate, bodily continuity the second, and rhythmic evolution the third;
- make chapters multi-track zones instead of assigning a new chapter/function to each successive track.

**User approval:** “Do what you recommend”

**Status:** `APPROVED — REMOVE` — explicit multi-track scope approved.

**Resolution:**

The three later tracks are removed from the canonical opening/current journey. Marauder remains provisional and unprotected. The opening resets to one track and future Tracks 2–3 remain Ground by default until a coherent multi-track vibe exists.

This resolution does not claim that Some Letters, Alive or Drift C are globally bad tracks or globally incompatible with STRANGE GAIT; it rejects their current opening placements and the four-track sequence they formed.
