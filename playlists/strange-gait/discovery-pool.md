# STRANGE GAIT — Discovery Pool

This is a **non-canonical inspiration layer**.

Its purpose is to preserve music worth knowing about even when that music is not admitted to the canonical STRANGE GAIT Spotify sequence.

A discovery-pool entry is not an ADD, does not change Spotify, does not prove neighbour compatibility, and does not bypass `constitution.md`, `rejected.md`, `revisit.md`, or listener-feedback authority.

## Why this exists

The editorial engine should help discover music, not turn every useful find into a binary playlist decision.

A run can therefore be successful in several different ways:

- add a track to the canonical playlist;
- identify a strong but unresolved placement hypothesis;
- uncover a track worth knowing even though its current playlist placement fails;
- find an artist, label, release, or aesthetic corridor worth following;
- create a useful set-direction hypothesis without changing the playlist.

The canonical playlist remains stricter than this pool.

## Entry classes

- **EXACT TRACK** — exact Spotify identity is resolved. May link to Spotify. This says only that the identity is known and the music is worth retaining as inspiration.
- **RESEARCH LEAD** — source-backed track/release/artist/label worth following, but exact Spotify identity, release status, tempo, or other required candidate evidence is incomplete. Do not invent a Spotify link.
- **RELEASE WATCH** — upcoming or newly announced release worth checking when it becomes available. Not a candidate until normal evidence and identity gates pass.

## Pool rules

1. Do not remove an entry merely because it fails the canonical playlist.
2. Do not promote an entry to the ledger from this file alone.
3. `REJECT` in `rejected.md` remains authoritative for its documented scope.
4. `PARKED` / `TRIGGERED` state in `revisit.md` remains authoritative for canonical reconsideration.
5. A pooled exact track may be discussed as music worth hearing without becoming user homework.
6. A RESEARCH LEAD or RELEASE WATCH must remain visibly unresolved; never invent identity, BPM, availability, or sonic facts.
7. Avoid duplicate entries. Update an existing row when materially new evidence appears.
8. Preserve why the item is interesting independently of whether it fits the current playlist slot.

## Relaxation-first rule

This file exists to give the user **more music with less management**.

Do not ask the user to rank, A/B test, confirm, or administrate the pool. Surface useful discoveries conversationally and let natural interest decide what gets revisited later.
