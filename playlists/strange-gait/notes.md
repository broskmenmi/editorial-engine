## Whole-journey placement policy

EXPLORE is whole-journey by default. Do not default discovery, scout targeting, candidate evaluation, or sequencing to appending after Stardancer merely because Stardancer is currently last or because the trio is listener-protected.

For every genuinely interesting resolved candidate, consider the full canonical journey and identify the strongest one or more plausible insertion regions from the evidence. A candidate may enter before the current first track, between any non-frozen/non-protected neighbours, or after the current last track. The tail is one option, not the default.

The protected objects are only the two internal handoffs `Transparency → When We Froze` and `When We Froze → Stardancer`. Do not insert between those pairs. `Truth in Noise → Transparency` is explicitly unprotected and remains a lawful candidate boundary. The three-track block may also move intact later if stronger long-form evidence supports that, subject to listener-feedback rules.

Do not reject or PARK a candidate solely because one preselected slot fails when another reasonable non-protected placement has not been considered. A placement-specific discovery may stay placement-specific only when the evidence or user request genuinely makes that slot the point of the test.

Scout requests in EXPLORE should therefore describe the target as whole-journey belonging/placement discovery rather than `Track 13 after Stardancer`, and each lead should carry one or more honest proposed placements chosen from the current journey rather than inheriting one global append slot.
