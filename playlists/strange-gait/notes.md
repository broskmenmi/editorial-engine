## Identity

**The body understands before the pattern does.**

STRANGE GAIT preserves the deepest principle learned from GROOVE OVER NOISE—bodily movement outranks spectacle—but permits noticeable rhythmic displacement.

The core distinction is not “more experimental techno.” It is **physical techno whose pattern bends without losing the body**.

## Story model

`Ground → Tilt → Lock → Fracture → Deep Lock → Escape`

No dominant summit is required. Narrative pressure comes from orientation, displacement and re-locking rather than simply from intensity.

## Initial scouting priority

Do not search for three seed tracks at once.

The first run should search outward for one opener that:

1. belongs immediately in the STRANGE GAIT world;
2. establishes bodily trust;
3. already contains some rhythmic crookedness or latent displacement;
4. avoids IDM cleverness, industrial impact, hardgroove cliché and melodic/vocal spectacle;
5. can plausibly support future tilt rather than spending the concept in Track 1.

For an empty ledger, `bootstrap opener — track 1 in empty ledger` is a valid concrete placement and neighbour compatibility is `N/A — empty ledger`.

## Volume boundary

GROOVE OVER NOISE remains intact and playable. Its saturation history informed this doctrine but is not copied into STRANGE GAIT persistent state.

Past near-misses such as half-time dub space, polymetric motion, organic percussion and strange gait are conceptual evidence only. Any exact track must be freshly discovered or materially re-evidenced here.

## Visual direction

Black and graphite base; off-grid architectural lines; restrained bone/steel typography; bruised violet for displacement; oxidized copper only for fractures or unresolved discussions. Avoid neon cyberpunk, generic waveform graphics, festival imagery and literal walking/feet motifs.
