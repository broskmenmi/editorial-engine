# STRANGE GAIT — Live-Mixing Doctrine

Live performance is a separate directed graph. It must never silently rewrite the canonical ordered-listening ledger, Spotify order, protected state, or frozen discussion regions.

## Edge characters

- `ANCHOR` — restore or preserve bodily orientation.
- `TILT` — introduce asymmetry or displacement without losing pulse.
- `LOCK` — deepen physical trust inside an altered gait.
- `FRACTURE` — make a deliberate rhythmic logic change while preserving a route forward.
- `ESCAPE` — simplify, release, reset, or exit without reopening the whole journey.
- `EMERGENCY EXIT` — recover when a route is not working.

## Evidence

Use BPM, key, phrase, mix regions, microtiming, metrical relation, rhythmic/spectral density, vocal collision risk, trajectory and listener evidence only when sourced. Direction matters: A → B is not assumed equal to B → A.

A documented half-time or double-time relation may make a numerically distant edge viable. Never invent such a relation from BPM arithmetic alone.

## Identity gate

Every destination must belong to STRANGE GAIT independently of live compatibility. A technically mixable transition cannot rescue a track that fails the playlist constitution.

## Output

For a live request return a small ranked set of exits with edge character, sourced evidence, chapter effect, principal risk and confidence. Do not produce one opaque “best next track.”
