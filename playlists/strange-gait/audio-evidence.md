# STRANGE GAIT — Audio Evidence Contract

Audio analysis is evidence, never a verdict. Accept only lawful audio files the user owns or may analyse, explicit exports from DJ tools, or documented lawful measurements.

Spotify metadata is not raw audio and does not expose swing, microtiming, phrase structure, density, perceived pulse, or waveform analysis.

## Authority

1. explicit user instruction and volunteered listener experience;
2. STRANGE GAIT constitution and approved editorial state;
3. sourced lawful audio measurements/model outputs;
4. craft convention and editorial hypothesis.

## Required provenance

Every stored field must preserve exact identity, source type/name, tool/version when applicable, timestamp, evidence class (`measured` or `model-derived`), confidence/reliability, units/scale where relevant, and method notes when needed.

Unknown values remain absent.

## Particularly useful STRANGE GAIT evidence

Track-level evidence may include:

- BPM and beat-grid confidence;
- alternative metrical interpretations;
- microtiming and swing estimates;
- kick/snare/hat onset relationships;
- syncopation descriptors;
- polymeter/polyrhythm evidence;
- phrase/section boundaries;
- rhythmic-density trajectory;
- spectral density and low-end occupancy;
- vocal presence;
- possible mix-in/mix-out regions;
- half-time/double-time relation evidence;
- embedding/fingerprint similarity.

Transition evidence may include directional perceived-pulse relation, tempo adjustment, phrase alignment, overlap region, rhythmic collision risk, spectral collision risk and confidence.

Model-derived “complexity,” “groove,” “energy,” “danceability,” “emotion,” genre, or compatibility remains model-derived and never becomes listener truth.

## Registry

`audio-evidence.json` stores machine-readable evidence. Importing evidence does not mutate the ledger, Spotify, acceptance state, or listener-review state.
