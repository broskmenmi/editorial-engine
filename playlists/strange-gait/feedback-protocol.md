# STRANGE GAIT — Listener Feedback Protocol

This file governs negative or uncertain listener feedback about accepted STRANGE GAIT tracks and transitions. It overrides conflicting complaint-handling instructions elsewhere.

## Core rule

A **complaint is evidence, not authorization**.

Statements such as “too weird,” “I lose the beat,” “too straight,” “too busy,” “this feels clever,” “the transition throws me out,” “I always skip it,” or “could this work somewhere else?” open a discussion. They do not authorize a playlist edit.

Exact commands such as “remove it,” “move it after X,” or “replace it with Y” authorize exactly the named action and scope.

## Clarification gate

Unless an exact action was explicitly ordered:

1. Freeze the affected region.
2. Preserve the user's wording in `under-review.md` as `AWAITING CLARIFICATION`.
3. Keep ledger and Spotify membership/order unchanged in that region.
4. Explain the original editorial role plainly.
5. Ask at most three short questions answerable from memory; never assign listening homework.
6. Distinguish the track itself, transition in, transition out, rhythmic gait, placement, and broader style.
7. Summarize the shared diagnosis before proposing KEEP, MOVE, REPLACE, or REMOVE.
8. If a solution changes any neighbour or additional track, name the complete before/after sequence and obtain explicit approval.

## Useful clarification questions

Choose only what is needed:

- Do you lose the bodily pulse, or do you simply notice the rhythm changing?
- Is the problem the whole track, the way it enters, or the way it leaves?
- Does the strange rhythm feel physical, or does it make you start analysing it?
- Once the track settles, do you want it to continue?
- Should we try moving it before considering removal?

## STRANGE GAIT-specific evidence

Direct reports such as “my body loses the beat,” “I start counting,” “it suddenly feels like IDM,” or “the weirdness makes me move more” are high-value listener evidence because they address the constitution directly.

Repeated skipping, stress, relief when a track ends, or loss of bodily pulse are strong evidence, but they still do not authorize removal without an explicit action command.

## States

- `AWAITING CLARIFICATION`
- `DIAGNOSIS AGREED`
- `APPROVED — KEEP`
- `APPROVED — MOVE`
- `APPROVED — REPLACE`
- `APPROVED — REMOVE`

Only an approved state may change the canonical ledger or Spotify for complaint-driven work.

## Scope boundary

A complaint about one track authorizes no bridge, new replacement search, neighbouring reorder, or chapter redesign unless the user explicitly approves that wider scope.

Other unfrozen regions may continue normal discovery while one region is awaiting clarification.
